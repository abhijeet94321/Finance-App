# **App Name**: Saldo

## Core Features:

- Initial Wealth Configuration: Establish a financial baseline by setting starting balances for all bank accounts, credit cards, and physical cash upon onboarding.
- Penny-Perfect Real-time Balance: Synchronize and update the total balance of every account and cash wallet in real-time as transactions are logged, ensuring every cent is tracked.
- Hybrid Transaction Entry: Log online payments and cash flow with distinct markers for total transparency across digital and physical spending.
- AI Spending Strategist: An AI advisor that acts as a tool to analyze your purchase history and reason through potential areas for spend reduction.
- Persistent Ledger: Centralized storage for all financial records ensuring data durability and instant accessibility for long-term tracking.
- Automated Monthly Audits: One-click generation of comprehensive spending reports for any billing cycle, providing instant real-time insights.
- Triple-Daily Entry Reminders: Receive push notifications three times a day as reminders to log expenditures or income, ensuring no transaction is missed.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Sophisticated periwinkle (#9E9EFF) for a calming, 'cozy' yet trustworthy financial presence.
- Background: Deep, midnight-toned slate (#14111F) for a professional and secure aesthetic.
- Accent: Sharp, medium blue (#678FFE) for actionable elements like submit buttons and data points.
- Headline and numeric data font: 'Space Grotesk' (sans-serif) for high-tech, numerical clarity and readability.
- Body font: 'Inter' (sans-serif) for maximum legibility in lists and descriptions.
- Minimalist line icons for category markers to maintain a clean, distraction-free environment.
- A cozy, user-friendly landing dashboard featuring soft-rounded cards and a spacious grid that ensures easy reading of balances on the first page.
- Smooth, progressive loading for data visualizations and gentle transitions between navigation views.
